# Video script (30 seconds, English subtitles are required)

Record the screen at 1280×720 or larger. Open the live site before recording. Use the date that works well on **your real data** (find it in advance: choose a place near the equator, slide the date until Energy turns from ! to ✓; write the dates down).

| Time | On screen | Subtitle (English) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Start screen, press **Start**. The 3D Moon appears and turns slowly. | "Where is it safe to land on the Moon? Lunar Navigator answers with a traffic light." |
| 4–11 s | Click **Apollo 12**. Camera flies in, the report opens and turns red: "Surveyor 3: 157 m away, inside the keep-out zone". | "Apollo 12 landed 157 m from Surveyor 3. NASA's 2011 rule says stay 2 km away: red." |
| 11–17 s | Type a free spot (for example `0, 10`) in the box. The report opens: Energy, Communication, Neighbors, each with ✓ ! ✕. Move the mouse over the Moon: the ring changes colour. | "Any spot gets three checks: sunlight, view of Earth, and neighbours. Colour, icon and word." |
| 17–24 s | Drag the **date slider** forward about a week (or press **Play**). The light on the Moon changes, the Energy block goes from yellow ! to green ✓ (or the reverse). | "Change the date: the Sun moves, the phase changes, and so does the answer." |
| 24–28 s | Press **View from Earth**, then **Set as A / B** to show the comparison table with the best value highlighted. | "Compare places side by side." |
| 28–30 s | Back to the Moon. | "Data: NASA LOLA, JPL, NASA 2011. Thresholds are our own choice. Built with AI help (Claude)." |

Notes
- Optional last second: show the **Save PDF** button, or the light theme button. Skip the tour question: press "No, thanks" when it appears (or record after the first visit; the site remembers the answer).
- Do **not** claim "Sun behind a mountain": terrain data is not included yet (the report says "Flat horizon").
- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** (anyone with the link can open it), check the link in a private window.
- On Mac: Cmd + Shift + 5 → "Record Selected Portion". On Windows: Win + G (Xbox Game Bar) or OBS.
- Subtitles: YouTube Studio → Subtitles → add English, paste the lines above with the times.
